Transit searches of nearby K and M dwarfs and of TOI hosts in TESS data

Independent searches of NASA TESS light curves for small transiting planets. One search combines the newest TESS sectors (97–105) with every earlier sector of nearby K and M dwarfs. Another looks for additional planets around the hosts of TESS Objects of Interest (TOIs) after masking the known TOIs. A full-sky search covers FGKM dwarfs brighter than T = 13.5.

These are planet candidates, not confirmed planets. Each passes automated and pixel-level vetting, but TESS alone cannot rule out every false-positive scenario. Ground-based photometry, high-resolution imaging, or radial velocities are needed to confirm or reject them. (Wording from the repository README.)

Search coverage

Figures quoted from the README at the time this page was written; they are not computed from the CSV files and the README is the current source.

  • Full-sky search: 4,039,203 FGKM dwarfs searched (TIC 8.2, T ≤ 13.5, Teff ≤ 6,500 K, R ≤ 1.5 R☉), using TESS-SPOC, QLP and SPOC light curves from Sectors 1–104.
  • Earlier searches: 9,979 nearby K and M dwarfs (T ≤ 11.5), 6,430 TOI hosts, 22,448 fainter M dwarfs (11.5 < T ≤ 12.5) and 84,863 M dwarfs with 12.5 < T ≤ 13.5.
  • The README also records known data gaps (for example QLP light curves dropped by an earlier version of the reader, and a QLP timing error in later sectors); see the top of the README.
